# NexGenAI — Getting Started Guide

Welcome to NexGenAI! This guide walks you through logging in, exploring the three operating surfaces, and testing all interactive labs.

---

## Step 1: Create an Account or Log In
1. Open `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Click **Get Started** to create an authentic student account.
3. Select your starting academic stage (e.g. **Class 11** for Career Discovery, or **College Year 3** for Industry Prep).
4. Complete the 4-step onboarding wizard to initialize your Career Passport.

---

## Step 2: Explore the Three Product Surfaces
Use the surface switcher in the top navigation bar:
- **Student OS**: Access your personal Dashboard, 30 Domains, Coding Lab, System Design Canvas, SOC Simulator, and AI Career Mentor.
- **College / TPO OS**: View active placement drives, test the automated student eligibility rules engine, and review batch placement analytics.
- **Recruiter OS**: Search for vetted candidates by verified skills and readiness score, and extend official job offers.

---

## Step 3: Test Interactive Features
- **Voice Engine**: In any course lesson or in AI Career Mentor, click **Listen** to hear natural speech narration, or click **Speak** to dictate your question using your microphone.
- **Coding Lab**: Navigate to **Practice → Coding Lab**, solve the "Two Sum" problem, and click **Run & Submit**.
- **System Design**: Go to **Prepare → System Design**, add components to the canvas, and click **Analyze Architecture**.
- **SOC Simulator**: Go to **Prepare → SOC Simulator**, investigate the SSH brute force authentication logs, and submit a containment report.
- **Resume ATS**: Upload or draft your resume in **Prepare → Resume & ATS** to evaluate keyword alignment against job descriptions.
